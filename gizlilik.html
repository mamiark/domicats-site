Privacy Policy

Domicats: Cat Domino Puzzle · Effective date: 10 November 2026

In short: Domicats does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no purchases with real money. Your progress stays on your device. If you email us, we use your email only to reply to you.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress (the stars you earned on each level, the in-game paws and hints you have, the items you own and have placed in the room) and your settings (sound on or off) in a file on your device. This file is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Internet

The game has no online features and does not send anything over the Internet. Three buttons in the settings panel (the gear icon) take you outside the game: "Privacy" and "Licenses" open pages of this website in your web browser, and "Contact us" opens your email app with a new message to us (its subject line contains the game version and platform). Your browser and email app handle these under their own policies.

Third parties

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity's hardware statistics and analytics are turned off, and the game contains no advertising, analytics or other third-party SDKs that collect data.

Device backups

Your device may include the game's save file in your own device or cloud backup (for example iCloud), depending on your settings. That backup belongs to your account; we have no access to it.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes your progress. We do not hold any data about you, unless you have emailed us (see below).

If you email us

If you write to us, for example with the "Contact us" button, we receive your email address, your message and the game version and platform in the subject line. We use them only to answer you and delete the email when it is no longer needed. You can ask us at any time to delete it.

Children

The game does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
